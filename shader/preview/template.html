Tune the overhaul

Every setting here is a QuietElements config option, and the right panel updates as you drag. Copy config gives you text to paste over the matching sections of BepInEx/config/claude.quietelements.cfg; the game picks up the change without a restart.

What each element spawns

Every particle system and light in each effect prefab, read from valheim_Data/StreamingAssets/SoftRef/Bundles/c4210710. Rates and sizes are per effect before the hitbox scaling. Brass numbers are the mod's values with the current settings.

Taken directly from the game

  • Textures, including the 8×8 fire flipbook
  • Emission rates, particle caps, lifetimes, start sizes, speeds and gravity
  • Color-over-life and size-over-life curves, sampled at 24 points
  • Emitter shapes, velocity, force, rotation and trail settings
  • Glow light color, intensity and range
  • Creature meshes, textures, hitbox sizes and the radius × 2 effect scaling

Approximated

  • Valheim's custom particle shaders are recreated with a simple model: texture × color × tint, plus emission
  • Lit smoke and frost clouds use flat scene lighting instead of the game's deferred lighting
  • Bloom and ACES tonemapping are close to the game's post-processing, but not identical
  • Noise turbulence on embers is a simple flow field
  • No soft-particle fading, no sound, and the target stands still in its T-pose

How the overhaul stacks

  • Frost changes the surface: a cold tint, a crust on upward faces, cracked-ice lines and slow glints. Flakes fall.
  • Fire glows in thin seams with soot between, and flame tongues lick up the silhouette using the game's own flame flipbook. Embers rise.
  • Poison pulses through wandering veins and gives a sickly tint. Drips fall.
  • Lightning is intermittent: jagged arcs crawl over a few patches in short bursts, and the light flashes in sync.
  • Spirit owns the rim, with wisps rising off the edges.
  • Each element uses a different channel and motion, so several at once still read. Stacked elements share one brightness and particle budget and one light.
  • Layers floating just above the skin carry flame tongues, an ice coat, toxic haze, stray arcs and a ghostly aura.
  • Char spreads the longer something burns, and frost creeps over the body after it lands.
